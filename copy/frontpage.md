## The lede

Leave the `lede` block EMPTY to fall back to the generated three-clause
line. Write one sentence in it to lead the page yourself.

The lede is yours most weeks, and one sentence is the whole job. Product's
ruling, 2026-08-11: a machine cannot be punchy here without ranking, and a
human can. Every rule design proposed for picking a channel to lead with
compares different units, so it selects the smallest unit rather than the
biggest story, and a stated rule makes that choice predictable rather than
neutral. Leading with something is itself the claim.

So the generated form is the FALLBACK for a week nobody writes one, and on
that week calm and concrete is the correct output. It is not the normal
case and should not read as one.

Two things the generated fallback holds that a written lede must hold too:

- No comparison between channels. Each figure is measured against its own
  place's own record, and none of them share a scale.
- "At least" on any heat count. The counts are floors under D-112, and
  dropping the qualifier for rhythm makes it a claim we cannot support.

## lede

**Paris has had 15 times more hot days this summer than its 1961-1990
average.**
