chart on that city's page, and the build REFUSES to publish a city whose

reader returning to the page. It is not an errata log; it is the page
explaining itself.

## palma

measured against a bar this station could not support: our threshold uses
a city's own July and August temperatures from 1971 to 2000, and Palma's
record starts in 1978, so seven of those thirty years do not exist here.
A part-length window drawn from the cooler end set the bar too low. Palma
now uses the complete 1991 to 2020 normal, the bar rises from 33.0 °C to
33.6 °C, and 2026 no longer leads.

The rank beside it reads second of 49, behind 2022.
