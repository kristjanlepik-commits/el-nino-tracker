## rung_note

Three of five models make a +4.0 peak more likely than not. Two produce no
member that reaches it at all. **They do not disagree about how likely it
is; they disagree about whether it is possible.**

So {p40}% is not an uncertain estimate. **It falls in a gap between 0 and
{nearest} that no model occupies.**

We added the rung because +3.5 has climbed from {p35_then} to {p35_now}
since July, and the rungs above it keep topping out.
