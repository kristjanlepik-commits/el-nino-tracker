## headline

Barcelona's wettest day in its complete record

## body

On 3 October **159.1 mm** of rain fell on Barcelona, the most in a single
day since its complete record began in 1978. Over three days it took
**238.2 mm**, also a record.

Its older record, back to 1920, has gaps. Even there, nothing wetter fell
in a day since December 1971, and only February 1944 beat the three days.

Two days earlier Tortosa, down the coast, had its wettest day since 1943, in a
complete record going back to 1920: 179.2 mm. Thirty kilometres inland, two gauges caught under 5 mm.

Elsewhere, far less. Valencia Airport had 28 mm, Albacete 1 mm.

The sea beside it had just had **its warmest September in a record going
back to 1940**, across the western and central Mediterranean.
