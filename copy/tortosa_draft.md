## headline

Tortosa's wettest day in 83 years

## body

On 1 October, **179.2 mm** of rain fell on Tortosa in a single day. Nothing
wetter has been recorded there since 1943.

Gauges 21 km away caught 24 mm. This was one storm cell, and it sat on one
town.

Three days later Barcelona took **159.1 mm** in a day, its most since
December 1971, and 238.2 mm over three.

Elsewhere, almost nothing. Valencia Airport 28 mm. Albacete 1 mm.

The sea that fed it had just had **its warmest September on record** across
the western and central Mediterranean.

*Tortosa's record has one gap, January to June 1957. No spring day there
since 1920 has passed 137.2 mm.*
