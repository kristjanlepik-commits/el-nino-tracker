How big does this one get?

That a strong-to-super event is coming is settled. What is still open is its peak magnitude, and the ocean is now the better evidence than the forecast.

01  ·  Observed

Subsurface heat is above the complete seasonal peak of both super-event analogs, with three months of build still ahead. +2.96 °C in July, against 1997 topping out at +2.56 in October and 2015 at +1.97 in August.
