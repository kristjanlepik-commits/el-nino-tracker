How hot has the European summer been?

Every city measured against its own record, never against each other. A hot day is one at or above that city's own 95th percentile for July and August, so 41.2 °C in Seville and 31.4 °C in Berlin. 14 of the 21 have had more of them than in any year on record. A typical year produces 0.
