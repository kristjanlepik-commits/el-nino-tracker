Spain wildfires, 2026 season

Season is open. Week 29 of a Mediterranean fire season that peaks in August.

Nothing on this page is added up, and there is no total. The figures below are different categories measured by different bodies: what burned, what the response cost, who was moved, what other events were estimated to cost, and what was granted afterwards. Summing them would count the same event more than once and would produce a number that measures nothing. Each section says what kind of quantity it holds before the first figure appears.

What nobody has counted

These carry the same weight as the figures above. A ledger that showed only what has been measured would read as the cost of the event, and it is not.

No estimator covers this hazard here
Insured losses
See absence_meaning. Three separate mechanisms exclude this hazard and territory.

Why there is no insured-loss figure. No public estimator produces an insured-loss figure for Mediterranean wildfire. PERILS covers neither wildfire as a European peril nor Spain as a territory. Spain's Consorcio de Compensacion de Seguros does not class wildfire as an extraordinary risk. France's CatNat scheme excludes forest fire by statute. Silence here says nothing whatsoever about the size of the event.

Guardrail. This comparison crosses categories and is legitimate ONLY because both sides are named and neither is summed. Written as 'the fires cost EUR 3.3 billion' it is a category error. The naming is what makes the claim true. Editor's standard, research/editorial_standards.md section 9.
