The expectation moves because the units get finer, not because the weather changed. That is why a sub-national map needs this comparison drawn on it every week: without it, eighty-one places at record lows reads as a catastrophe rather than as an ordinary dekad.

Each sub-national unit holds a 26-year record for this dekad, so in any ordinary period roughly one unit in 26 sets a new low. Across 2,122 units that is 82 places, and 81 were observed. The same dekad expects about 5 at country level, where there are only 123 units to break records.

The comparison still matters where it is uncomfortable. Turkiye ranks 23 of 26 nationally, in the better half, while Gaziantep, Mardin, Sanliurfa and Sirnak are each at their worst on record. A country-level read would have filed this as fine.

In 62 of 123 countries the driver is identified as water, so those can be called dry. In the remaining 61 the honest sentence stops at below its own record, with no driver named.
