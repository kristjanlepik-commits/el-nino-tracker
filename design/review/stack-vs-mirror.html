Stacked or mirrored

Paris, every summer since 1950. Same two numbers both times: days above 31.8 °C and nights above 20 °C, counted to the same date each year.

Stacked

solid hot days   faded hot nights   tallest bar 2026, 30 + 17 = 47

Reads as one rising quantity, which is the appeal. But the height is days plus nights and those can be the same 24 hours: a day above 31.8 °C followed by a night above 20 °C is counted twice. The tallest bar says 47 of something, and there is no something.

Mirrored

above hot days   below hot nights   one scale, 4px per count on both halves

Nothing is summed, and the same count draws the same size above and below. What this shows and the stack hides: the two measures pick out the same two summers. 1976 stands alone in the old record on days and nights both, and 2026 beats it on both: 30 days against 17, 17 nights against 13. Two instruments agreeing on which summers mattered is harder to dismiss than either alone.
