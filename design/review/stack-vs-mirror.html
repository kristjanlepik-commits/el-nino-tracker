Stacked or mirrored

Paris, every summer since 1950. Same two numbers both times: days above 31.8 °C and nights above 20 °C, counted to the same date each year.

Stacked

solid hot days   faded hot nights   tallest bar 2026, 30 + 17 = 47

Reads as one rising quantity, which is the appeal. The bar height is days plus nights, and those can be the same 24 hours: a day above 31.8 °C followed by a night above 20 °C counts twice. So the tallest bar says 47 of something, and there is no something.

Mirrored

above hot days   below hot nights   one shared scale

Nothing is summed. The extra thing it shows: the nights half stays empty for decades while the days half is already busy, so the two measures diverge in TIME as well as size. That is invisible when they are stacked into one height.
