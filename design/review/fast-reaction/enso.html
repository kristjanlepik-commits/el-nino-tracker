What this is

An anomaly. How far the Niño 3.4 region sits above or below its own 1991-2020 average, in degrees Celsius, for one three-month season.

What this is not

Not a forecast, and not a probability. This is the observed state of one index for one season; what it implies for the winter peak is the weekly brief's question, not this one.
