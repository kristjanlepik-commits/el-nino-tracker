Days above the line, nights below, sharing one scale so a taller bar really does mean more. They are NOT stacked: a hot day and a hot night can be the same 24 hours, so a combined height would be a number rather than a measurement. The nights half is empty for decades because it genuinely was: 36 of 77 years recorded no tropical night at all in Paris. This year there have been 17, which is 1 of 77. No multiple is quoted for nights, because a ratio against a baseline of about one a year would be arithmetic rather than evidence.

And the hottest day of each summer

The hottest single day of each summer, to the same date. 2026 is the 2nd hottest, not the hottest. It reached 40.6 °C against 41.9 °C in 2019, which is the open ring. Paris has had more hot days this summer than in any year on record and its hottest day was still not a record: a count and a peak are different claims, and neither borrows the other's rank.
