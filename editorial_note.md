This is the week the official forecast caught up. CPC re-issued its strength table for the first time since mid-May, and it jumped hard toward where the dynamical models have been sitting for weeks. The whole ladder moved up with it: a very strong event is now near-certain, a 1997 or 2015-magnitude winter sits at four in five, and the chance of a peak beyond anything in the instrumental record climbed to two in three. For two months the story here was the gap between CPC's caution and the models' alarm. This week CPC closed most of it. The +3.0°C line is still the least-anchored number on the page, but it is no longer the models alone saying it.
