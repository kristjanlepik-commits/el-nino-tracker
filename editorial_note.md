The top of the ladder just moved, and it is the biggest shift since the tracker launched. With this issue the headline follows a multi-model consensus, weighting the SEAS5 and NMME forecasts that have run hot for weeks over CPC's slower monthly table. A 1997 or 2015-magnitude winter, a peak above +2.5°C, now reads at roughly two in three. And a rung that did not exist last week appears above it: a peak beyond anything in the instrumental record, above +3.0°C, at nearly two in five. Treat that top number with care; it is the least-anchored figure here and leans almost entirely on the models. But subsurface heat, the strongest wind bursts, and the full forecast pool are all telling the same story now, and it is not a quiet one.
