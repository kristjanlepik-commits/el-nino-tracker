**The wind is ahead of the surface.**

A fresh westerly burst has run through October, arriving faster than at the same date in 1997 or 2015. The surface has not followed yet. Niño 3.4 rose half a degree from early September to early October and has added only a tenth since. That is not a stall. The warmth the winds push east travels below the surface and comes up weeks later, and the heat stored there in September was the most since records began in 1979. Both earlier events warmed in November after October bursts: 2015 at Niño 3.4, 1997 further east. This year is already hottest in the east. If it follows them, November is when the surface shows it, most likely there first.
