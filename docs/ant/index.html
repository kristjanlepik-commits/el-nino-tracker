How much will the world spend on AI?

People and companies spent about $7bn on AI in 2024. Today they spend at a rate of about $193bn a year: subscriptions to ChatGPT and Claude, AI built into software, and AI doing work.

Everything paid for AI models and AI work a year, to anyone, whoever ends up with the money. An estimate: Anthropic's audited and OpenAI's reported revenue, scaled up for the other labs and for spending that never reaches a lab. 2 of 4  ·  Data centres

Will the money for data centres keep coming?

Investors' money buys computing power. The West's AI data centres had about 5 gigawatts of it at the end of 2024 and about 12 a year later. By the end of this year they will have about 25 gigawatts. One gigawatt is roughly what a large nuclear reactor produces.

AI data-centre capacity in the US and the rest of the West, without China: gigawatts of computing power at the end of each year. Every point is an estimate: 2024 and 2025 from Epoch's chip counts, 2026 from Epoch's database of sites. The two futures are from the AI - Compute model. In a funding crash, building slows in 2028 and 2029 and partly catches up in 2030.
