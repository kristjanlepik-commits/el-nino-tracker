France, the UK, and Spain are each at their highest fire week in the 15-year satellite record

VIIRS satellite fire detections for the calendar week Jul 19-25, in every year of the single-sensor record (SNPP, 2012-2026), including today: Europe's Jul 25 satellite day completed processing this afternoon. Each country is compared only to itself; the dashed line is its own 14-year mean for this week of the year.

Counts are 375 m VIIRS fire pixels (NASA FIRMS), SNPP satellite only for cross-year consistency, low-confidence detections excluded, points assigned to countries by national boundary polygons applied identically in all years. 2026 comes from the near-real-time product; 2012-2025 from the science-quality archive; NRT counts are typically revised slightly when the archive catches up, and Jul 25 may still gain a few late-arriving detections. Detections measure fire activity seen from orbit, not burned area and not severity. France's week is dominated by the Gironde/Landes complex; Spain's 2022 comparison bar is the Sierra de la Culebra summer. UK counts are small in absolute terms; its record is relative to a low base.
