How big does this El Niño get?

The Pacific has not been this warm beneath the surface in the 47 years we have measured it. August's reading is already above what 1997 reached at its October peak, two months later in its own season.

01 · Observed

Subsurface heat is at the highest value in the 47-year record. Above 1997's own October peak of +2.56, with roughly three months of seasonal build still ahead.
