Crops

Three of Yemen’s 20 crop regions are at a record low. One was the previous worst.

Four other countries also passed their own previous worst, which is a normal number in any given week. 69 crop regions worldwide are at their worst on record for this point in the season, against a typical 60, higher than all but three of the last twelve years. Where they cluster is what this page is about.

Newest observation: the ten days to 31 July. All 123 places are reporting at this dekad. The source publishes every ten days and has not published since, so this is the most recent reading available rather than a reading of today.

A season that has not opened cannot be measured, so this page says most about countries whose season is starting. 23 of the 123 countries measured here, about one in five, open a growing season between now and early November, among them Spain and Argentina. That is a calendar, not a forecast.

The same season, counted two ways

These croplands have been getting greener for twenty-five years. Leave that trend in and this season’s crop result is better than usual; take it out and it lands in the worse third.

Crop result across the 26 years of this dekad: 25 of 26 with the trend left in, 9 of 26 with it taken out. Neither is the correct one; they answer different questions.

This dekad against the whole record

Where each figure sits among the 26 years of this same dekad, across all 123 places we measure. Detrended is the figure to read: these instruments drift, and the drift is upward, so the raw rank flatters every stress claim. Both are shown because they disagree and a reader should see that there was a choice.

detrended raw
all five instruments available everywhere, read togetherRainfall, 3-month, Temperature, Vegetation, cumulative, Vegetation, current, Water satisfaction3 of 262 of 26

the purely meteorological instrumentsRainfall, 3-month, Temperature1 of 261 of 26

These are two named groups, not a split of the five: Vegetation, current and Water satisfaction belong to neither, being an instantaneous crop state and a modelled water balance. So the two lines cannot be read as "the weather" against "the crops" with nothing left over.

  • Detrending moves these two figures in opposite directions, so raw and detrended are both emitted and neither stands alone. Temperature worsens almost everywhere and cropland greens, so the raw gap between them is widened by two secular trends rather than by this season alone.
  • 0.5 is the mean of every place's own series by construction and is not a normal to compare against. Use the observed range across the record, which is what the series carries.
  • The crop outcome instrument is cumulative over the growing cycle, so at this dekad it integrates a season that has not finished. It is a reading of conditions to date and carries no statement about the rest of the season.

How we know 81 is an ordinary number

The expectation rises with the number of units, not with the weather, so any map at this resolution shows dozens of record lows every week. The figure marked is what an EVEN spread of records would give, and records do not fall evenly. The last 12 years of this dekad averaged 59.9 and ranged from 24 to 104, counted over the same places shown here.

This measurement is of the crop canopy and not of what stressed it: heat, drought, disease and late planting are not separable here, which is why no row on this page names a cause.
