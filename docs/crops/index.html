Crops · dekad 2026-07-11

Eight of Chad’s 22 crop regions are at a record low. Three was the previous worst.

Five other countries also passed their own previous worst, which is a normal number in any given week. 81 crop regions worldwide are at their worst on record for this point in the season, against a typical 59, higher than all but two of the last twelve years. Where they cluster is what this page is about.

A season that has not opened cannot be measured, so this page says most about countries whose season is starting. 25 of the 123 countries measured here, about one in five, open a growing season between now and early November, among them Spain and Argentina. That is a calendar, not a forecast.

How we know 81 is an ordinary number

The expectation rises with the number of units, not with the weather, so any map at this resolution shows dozens of record lows every week. The figure marked is what an EVEN spread of records would give, and records do not fall evenly. The last 12 years of this dekad averaged 59.2 and ranged from 25 to 105, counted over the same places shown here.

This measurement is of the crop canopy and not of what stressed it: heat, drought, disease and late planting are not separable here, which is why no row on this page names a cause.
