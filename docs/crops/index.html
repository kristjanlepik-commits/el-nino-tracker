Crops · dekad 2026-07-11

Eight of Chad’s 22 crop regions are at a record low. Three was the previous worst.

Five other countries are flagged this week. For the rest of the 123 we track, conditions are ordinary. 81 crop regions worldwide are at their worst on record for this point in the season, against a typical 59. Globally that is an ordinary week. What is not ordinary is where they cluster, and that is what this page is about.

A season that has not opened cannot be measured, so this page says most about countries whose season is starting. 25 of the 123 countries measured here, about one in five, open a growing season between now and early November, among them Spain and Argentina. That is a calendar, not a forecast.

How we know 81 is an ordinary number

The expectation rises with the number of units, not with the weather, so any map at this resolution shows dozens of record lows every week. The figure marked is what an EVEN spread of records would give, and records do not fall evenly. Against its own recent history this count is higher than all but 2 of the last 12 years, on a mean of 59.2 and a range of 25 to 105, counted over the same places shown here.
