One scale, three instruments
Every panel saturates at 2.5 standard deviations below that region's own normal, so a colour means the same distance-from-normal on all three. It does not mean the same consequence: these measure different things and move at different speeds. The figures under each heading stay in the instrument's own units. Atlantico Sur and Atlantico Norte are unshaded on water satisfaction: their crop water need has been met in every year of the baseline, so there is no spread to measure this year against.

A fourth instrument is not shown here. Cumulative vegetation, the crop-outcome measure closest to yield, reads 3rd of 26 for this country with one region at a record. It integrates from the start of the season, so it is structurally the last to move and reads calmest exactly when a fast deterioration is under way. It is left off the panels to keep three maps comparable in kind; it is on the page in full below.
