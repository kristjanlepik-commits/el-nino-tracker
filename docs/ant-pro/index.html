How this works and sources

The model. This page runs ai/compute-demand-2030's scenario model v4.1 (model/scenarios_v41.py) in your browser and reproduces it cell by cell. It combines five ways the AI market could split its money with five paths for how fast AI develops, plus how fast US data centres can be connected to the grid. That makes 125 combinations, weighted by your sliders. The defaults are Kristjan's adopted weights (statement 20261112-0105) and his view of a crash (ask 05: AI training is cut for everyone and stops where the cash cushion is thin; statement 20261112-1010).

Anthropic's value comes from mkt/anthropic-valuation's v4.1 grid (R-20261112-v41-grid-valuation). It assumes Anthropic takes a 35%, 45% or 55% share of frontier-lab revenue, interpolated in between. It discounts at 9.74%, counts a wiped-out equity at zero, and values takeoff on the usage the grid can serve at late-plateau prices, an interim method until the AGI module (F-20261112-takeoff-upside-weighted-63-today). Its per-scenario values assume grid-bound supply, so this page's weighted figure can differ by about $1 from mkt's own ($63.2 at 45%), which revalues each power setting. US jobs come from ai/jobs' v4.1 grid (F-20261112-jobs-grid-compute-v4-1-2030). Neither responds to the model-assumption sliders.

Data-centre verdict. This compares US AI demand in 2030 with energised US AI capacity:
  • shortage: 1.00 or more;
  • balanced: 0.85-1.00;
  • weak: 0.75-0.85, where owners earn but not enough to repay their debt;
  • oversupply: below 0.75.
The headline figure is weak and oversupply together (finding F-20261112-v41a-us-balanced-lean-on-kristjan-break).
Key anchors.
  • End-2026 AI capacity is about 30 GW of IT, 20 GW of it in the US (F-20261112-base-v41-30gw-ai-us-20).
  • The US grid has never connected more than about 10 GW of data-centre demand in a year (F-20261112-bnef-us-grid-record-10gw).
  • OpenAI plans $856bn of compute against $840bn of revenue over 2026-30 (F-20261112-openai-plan-856bn-compute-840bn-revenue).
  • Telecom traffic kept growing through the 2001-03 capex bust (F-20261112-telecom-traffic-grew-capex-fell-42pct).
Every other input is judgement.

Research, not a recommendation to buy, sell or subscribe. Values rest on scenario-model figures that are not yet verified. The best AGI cases are upside cases, not forecasts. The model was built in a Claude session, and Anthropic makes Claude.
