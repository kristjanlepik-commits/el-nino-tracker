2026 is 2nd of 69 for hot days.

Hot here means 31.5 °C or above, a level about one summer day in twenty used to reach at this station between 1971 and 2000. The bar has not moved; the number of days clearing it has. Every year is counted to 3 August, so a part-finished summer is never set against complete ones.

And the nights

3 nights so far that never dropped below 20 °C. No multiple is quoted here. Cologne averages about 0.2 a year, and dividing by a base that thin produces a large number and no evidence. 10 of the 24 cities are gated this way.

And the hottest day of each summer

Cologne's hottest day this year, 38.0 °C, is 3rd on this record. The previous best is 40.3 °C in 2019, the dot on the drop line. Its hot-day count is 2nd of 69: a count and a peak are different claims, and neither borrows the other's rank.
