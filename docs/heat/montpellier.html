Hot here means 33.6 °C or above, a level about one summer day in twenty used to reach at this station between 1971 and 2000. The bar has not moved; the number of days clearing it has. Every year is counted to 3 August, so a part-finished summer is never set against complete ones.

And the nights

41 nights so far at or above 20 °C, against 5.2 in a typical 1961-1990 summer by this date. That is 1st of 77 on this station's record.

And the hottest day of each summer

The hottest day of Montpellier's year, to the same date. 2026 is the 2nd hottest, not the hottest, at 40.8 °C against 43.5 °C in 2019, the dot on the drop line. Its hot-day count is the most on record and its hottest single day 2nd: a count and a peak are different claims, and neither borrows the other's rank.
