No multiple is published for Palma's days. The station opened after 1961, so its 1961-1990 baseline is part-length and drawn from the warmer end of the period. The count and the rank stand; the ratio is not emitted at all rather than emitted with a warning.

And the nights

58 nights so far at or above 20 °C, against 30.7 in a typical 1961-1990 summer by this date. That is 3 of 49 on this station's record.

And the hottest day of each summer

The hottest day of Palma's year, to the same date. 2026 is the 16th hottest, not the hottest, at 35.2 °C against 38.0 °C in 2003, the open ring. More hot days than any year on record and its hottest day still short of one: a count and a peak are different claims, and neither borrows the other's rank.
