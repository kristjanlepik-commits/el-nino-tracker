No 1961-1990 comparison is shown for Palma. This thermometer starts in 1978, so that baseline would cover only its warmer final years, and the figure would look like every other city's while meaning something weaker. The count and the rank are measured and stand.

Hot here means 33.0 °C or above, a level about one summer day in twenty used to reach at this station between 1971 and 2000. The bar has not moved; the number of days clearing it has. Every year is counted to 2 August, so a part-finished summer is never set against complete ones.

And the nights

58 nights so far that never dropped below 20 °C, against 30.7 in a typical 1961-1990 summer by this date. That is 3rd of its 49 summers.

And the hottest day of each summer

Palma's hottest day this year, 35.2 °C, is 16th on this record. The previous best is 38.0 °C in 2003, the dot on the drop line. Its hot-day count is the most on record: a count and a peak are different claims, and neither borrows the other's rank.
