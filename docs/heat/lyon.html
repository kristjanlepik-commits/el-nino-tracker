Hot here means 34.7 °C or above, a level about one summer day in twenty used to reach at this station between 1991 and 2020. The percentile thresholds on this page are computed over this period. Where it is not the default 1971-2000, the city's record does not cover that window and a complete later WMO normal is used instead, which yields a HIGHER threshold and so understates rather than overstates. The bar has not moved; the number of days clearing it has. Every year is counted to 15 August, so a part-finished summer is never set against complete ones. That threshold is Lyon's own. It is not a national standard and it is not comparable with another city's: every figure on this page is measured against this station and nothing else.
