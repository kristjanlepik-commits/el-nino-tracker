Hot here means 31.4 °C or above, a level about one summer day in twenty used to reach at this station between 1971 and 2000. The bar has not moved; the number of days clearing it has. Every year is counted to 20 August, so a part-finished summer is never set against complete ones. That threshold is Berlin's own. It is not a national standard and it is not comparable with another city's: every figure on this page is measured against this station and nothing else.
