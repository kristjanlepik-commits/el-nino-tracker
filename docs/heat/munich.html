2026 is the most on record for hot days. This station moved 3.1 km in 1997, so the record is not one continuous site and 'of 72' spans more than one.

Hot here means 30.7 °C or above, a level about one summer day in twenty used to reach at this station between 1971 and 2000. The bar has not moved; the number of days clearing it has. Every year is counted to 3 August, so a part-finished summer is never set against complete ones.

And the nights

6 nights so far that never dropped below 20 °C. No multiple is quoted here. Munich averages about 0.4 a year, and dividing by a base that thin produces a large number and no evidence. 10 of the 24 cities are gated this way.

And the hottest day of each summer

The hottest day of Munich's year, to the same date. 2026 is the hottest on this record too, at 37.6 °C. Both the count and the peak are records here, which is not true everywhere: a count and a peak are separate claims and each carries its own rank.
