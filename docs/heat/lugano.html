Hot here means 30.2 °C or above, a level about one summer day in twenty used to reach at this station between 1971 and 2000. The bar has not moved; the number of days clearing it has. Every year is counted to 12 August, so a part-finished summer is never set against complete ones. That threshold is Lugano's own. It is not a national standard and it is not comparable with another city's: every figure on this page is measured against this station and nothing else.

46 nights so far that never dropped below 20 °C, against 4.8 in a typical 1961-1990 summer by this date. That is 1st of its 162 summers.

Lugano's hottest day this year, 35.2 °C, is 14th on this record. The previous best is 38.0 °C in 1945, the dot on the drop line. A typical summer here peaks at 32.2 °C. Its hot-day count is the most on record by this date: a count and a peak are different claims, and neither borrows the other's rank.
