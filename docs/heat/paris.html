The Long Swell Heat Paris · ORLY · to 3 August 2026

Paris used to get 2 hot days by this point in the summer. This year: 30.

A hot day here means one at or above 31.8 °C, which is this station's own 95th percentile for July and August between 1971 and 2000. The bar for what counts as hot has not moved; the number of days clearing it has. Both figures below are counted to 3 August, in 2026 and in every earlier year, so a part-finished summer is never set against complete ones.

And the nights

17 nights so far that never dropped below 20 °C. No multiple is quoted here. Paris averages about 0.9 a year, and dividing by a base that thin produces a large number and no evidence. The count is published and the ratio is withheld. 8 of the 22 cities are gated this way.

And the hottest day of each summer

The hottest day of Paris's year, to the same date. 2026 is the 2nd hottest, not the hottest, at 40.6 °C against 41.9 °C in 2019, the dot on the drop line. Its hot-day count is the most on record and its hottest single day 2nd: a count and a peak are different claims, and neither borrows the other's rank.
