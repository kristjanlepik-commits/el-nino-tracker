Hot here means 27.9 °C or above, a level about one summer day in twenty used to reach at this station between 1971 and 2000. The bar has not moved; the number of days clearing it has. Every year is counted to 10 August, so a part-finished summer is never set against complete ones. That threshold is Nottingham's own. It is not a national standard and it is not comparable with another city's: every figure on this page is measured against this station and nothing else.

1 night so far that never dropped below 20 °C. No multiple is quoted here. Nottingham did not record a single one in the whole of 1961-1990 by this date, so there is no base to divide by. 24 of the 42 cities are gated this way.

Nottingham's hottest day this year, 34.8 °C, is 3rd on this record. The previous best is 39.8 °C in 2022, the dot on the drop line. A typical summer here peaks at 29.4 °C. Its hot-day count is 2nd of 69: a count and a peak are different claims, and neither borrows the other's rank.
