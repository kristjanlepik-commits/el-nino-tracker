HeatWeek of 3 August 2026

How hot has the European summer been?

At least 22 of these 42 European cities have had more hot days this summer than in any year on record.

These forty-two stations were chosen for where heat was expected rather than as a sample of the continent. A hot day means hot for that city: 41.2 °C in Seville, 23.8 °C in Aberdeen. Each is measured against its own thermometer and its own history, never against the others.

How far from normal, city by city

Each row is one city's entire record, one mark per summer. Basel's 41 hot days beat all 144 summers it has on file. Helsinki's 5 beat 50 in 100 of its own. A crowded row is just a longer record.

Hot days and hot nights are different summers
MarseilleIts most hot days on record, by ten days. For hot nights, 6th of 77.
Valencia7th of 89 for hot days. For hot nights, 2nd of 89.

If one measure could stand in for the other, these two would lean the same way. They lean opposite ways. 24 cities show no night figure at all. They average under two hot nights a year, and dividing by a base that small gives you a big number and no evidence.
