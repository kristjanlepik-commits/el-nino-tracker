How hot has the European summer been?

At least 22 of these 36 European cities have had more hot days this summer than in any year on record. In a typical year, that number is zero.

A hot day means hot for that city: 41.2 °C in Seville, 25.7 °C in Helsinki. Each is measured against its own thermometer and its own history, never against the others.

Hot days and hot nights are different summers
MarseilleIts most hot days on record, by seven days. For hot nights, 7th of 77.
Seville8th of 76 for hot days. For hot nights, 3rd of 76.

If one measure could stand in for the other, these two would lean the same way. They lean opposite ways. 19 cities show no night figure at all. They average under two hot nights a year, and dividing by a base that small gives you a big number and no evidence.
