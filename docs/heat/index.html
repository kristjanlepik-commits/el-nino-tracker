How hot has the summer been?

At least 26 of these 54 cities have had more hot days in their latest summer than in any year on record.

These fifty-four stations were chosen for where heat was expected rather than as a sample of the continent. A hot day means hot for that city: 41.2 °C in Seville, 23.8 °C in Aberdeen. Each is measured against its own thermometer and its own history, never against the others. Where a city's summer is already over, the latest complete one is the one counted.

Hot days and hot nights are different summers
ZagrebIts most hot days on record, by sixteen days. For hot nights, 25th of 146.
Parana69th of 69 for hot days. For hot nights, 9th of 69.

If one measure could stand in for the other, these two would lean the same way. They lean opposite ways. 28 cities show no night figure at all. They average under two hot nights a year, and dividing by a base that small gives you a big number and no evidence.
