How hot has the European summer been?

At least 23 of these 45 European cities have had more hot days this summer than in any year on record.

These forty-five stations were chosen for where heat was expected rather than as a sample of the continent. A hot day means hot for that city: 41.2 °C in Seville, 23.8 °C in Aberdeen. Each is measured against its own thermometer and its own history, never against the others.

Hot days and hot nights are different summers
ZagrebIts most hot days on record, by sixteen days. For hot nights, 25th of 146.
Seville6th of 76 for hot days. For hot nights, 2nd of 76.

If one measure could stand in for the other, these two would lean the same way. They lean opposite ways. 25 cities show no night figure at all. They average under two hot nights a year, and dividing by a base that small gives you a big number and no evidence.
