Murcia used to get 2 hot days by this point in the summer. This year: 14.

A hot day here means one at or above 38.6 °C, which is this station's own 95th percentile for July and August between 1971 and 2000. The bar for what counts as hot has not moved; the number of days clearing it has. Both figures below are counted to 2 August, in 2026 and in every earlier year, so a part-finished summer is never set against complete ones.

2026 is the most on record for hot days.

No multiple is published for Murcia's days. The station opened after 1961, so its 1961-1990 baseline is part-length and drawn from the warmer end of the period. The count and the rank stand; the ratio is not emitted at all rather than emitted with a warning.

And the nights

56 nights so far at or above 20 °C, against 9.4 in a typical 1961-1990 summer by this date. That is 1 of 43 on this station's record.

And the hottest day of each summer

The hottest day of Murcia's year, to the same date. 2026 is the 5th hottest, not the hottest, at 44.2 °C against 45.7 °C in 1994, the open ring. More hot days than any year on record and its hottest day still short of one: a count and a peak are different claims, and neither borrows the other's rank.
