Hot here means 28.3 °C or above, a level about one summer day in twenty used to reach at this station between 1971 and 2000. The bar has not moved; the number of days clearing it has. Every year is counted to 3 August, so a part-finished summer is never set against complete ones.

And the nights

The 20 °C night is a Mediterranean measure and Stockholm is outside its range. None this year, and one night in the whole of 1961-1990 by this date. No multiple is quoted, because dividing by a base that thin is arithmetic rather than evidence. 10 of the 24 cities are gated this way.

And the hottest day of each summer

Stockholm's hottest day this year, 30.6 °C, is 21st on this record. The previous best is 34.2 °C in 1994, the dot on the drop line. Its hot-day count is 19th of 76: a count and a peak are different claims, and neither borrows the other's rank.
