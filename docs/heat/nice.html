2026 is the most on record for hot days.

Hot here means 30.2 °C or above, a level about one summer day in twenty used to reach at this station between 1971 and 2000. The bar has not moved; the number of days clearing it has. Every year is counted to 3 August, so a part-finished summer is never set against complete ones.

And the nights

56 nights so far that never dropped below 20 °C, against 16.4 in a typical 1961-1990 summer by this date. That is 2nd of its 77 summers.

And the hottest day of each summer

Nice's hottest day this year, 33.5 °C, is 20th on this record. The previous best is 37.7 °C in 2006, the dot on the drop line. Its hot-day count is the most on record: a count and a peak are different claims, and neither borrows the other's rank.
