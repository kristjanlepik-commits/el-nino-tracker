The Long Swell Heat Frankfurt · Frankfurt/Main · to 3 August 2026

Frankfurt used to get 2 hot days by this point in the summer. This year: 23.

A hot day here means one at or above 32.1 °C, which is this station's own 95th percentile for July and August between 1971 and 2000. The bar for what counts as hot has not moved; the number of days clearing it has. Both figures below are counted to 3 August, in 2026 and in every earlier year, so a part-finished summer is never set against complete ones.

2026 is the most on record for hot days. This station moved 5.86 km in 2014, so the record is not one continuous site and 'of 86' spans more than one.

And the nights

15 nights so far that never dropped below 20 °C. No multiple is quoted here. Frankfurt averages about 0.3 a year, and dividing by a base that thin produces a large number and no evidence. The count is published and the ratio is withheld. 8 of the 22 cities are gated this way.

And the hottest day of each summer

The hottest day of Frankfurt's year, to the same date. 2026 is the hottest on this record too, at 41.1 °C. Both the count and the peak are records here, which is not true everywhere: a count and a peak are separate claims and each carries its own rank.
