How many young Europeans are dissatisfied with their democracy

From 9% in Denmark to 68% in Greece. This map shows how many people born 1997 or later are dissatisfied with how democracy works in their country: 34% on average across the 27 EU countries. That is not anger: we found no free survey that asks about anger by age in every EU country. The newest answers were collected by 1 April 2026, so this autumn's protests are not in it.

What this can and cannot say

  • Stops before the autumn 2026 protests.
  • Covers nationals and EU citizens resident in each country; young people from outside the EU are not in it.
  • Measures dissatisfaction with democracy, not anger. We found no free survey that asks about anger by age in every EU country.

Not very or not at all satisfied with the way democracy works in their country. Standard Eurobarometer 103, 104, 105, Volume C. Fieldwork 26 Mar to 10 May 2025, 9 Oct to 5 Nov 2025, 12 Mar to 1 Apr 2026.
